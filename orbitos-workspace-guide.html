Ranks and halos

Everyone in a workspace has a rank. You can tell it at a glance from the coloured halo around their photo, on the org chart, in calls and in messages.

TM

Owner

Runs the workspace. Sees Restricted Vault documents and raises onboarding, move and offboarding tickets.

KN

Executive

Has the same powers as an Owner. Pro allows up to three.

NK

Manager

Can bring outside guests into calls and meetings, and appoint Team Leads.

SD

Team Lead

Leads a group. The first stop when someone escalates an Issue ticket.

AV

Member

Works on projects, chats, joins calls and raises Issue and Access tickets.

Internal Management sits outside the ranks. It's the small group (one to three seats, depending on plan) who hold the Admin Tools passcode, set ranks, wire the org chart and work the full ticket queue. Any rank can hold a seat.

Where a ticket goes

Every request shares one queue at /tickets. Issues climb the org chart one step at a time. Everything else goes straight to Internal Management.

An Issue, escalated

Each escalation moves it one level up. Internal Management can see it at every step and step in at any time.

Good to know

The behaviours people ask about most, in one place.

Reminders arrive the day before

Everyone assigned to a directive gets an email 24 hours before it's due, sent at 09:00 South African time.

Transcripts need a majority

A call is transcribed only if more than half the people in it agree, so in a two-person call both must say yes. Speech is captured in each person's own browser.

The Vault locks itself again

The passcode gate comes back every time the page loads. Wrong attempts are limited, and you can reset the passcode by email.

Restricted means restricted

Restricted documents are visible only to owners and whoever uploaded them. They never reach anyone else's browser.

Your calendar, anywhere

Subscribe to your private feed from Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar. If the link is ever shared by mistake, rotate it in Settings and the old one stops working.

Guests don't need an account

On paid plans, clients join calls from an invite link and answer meeting invites from a signed RSVP link. You're told as soon as they reply.

Email problems show up on the dashboard

If invites or reminders stop being delivered, owners see a banner on the dashboard instead of the problem staying hidden.

Lost a device?

Settings, then Security, has Sign out everywhere. It ends every session on every device at once.

Install it on your phone

Use Add to Home Screen in your browser. Calendar, messages and the team tree have layouts made for small screens, and calls ring with push notifications.

Light, dark or system

Pick a theme in Settings. If your device asks for less motion or less transparency, OrbitOS follows it: animations calm down and glass panels turn solid.
